# cmimc2025-14 variant

Let $n$ and $k$ be positive integers with $k \le n$. Form a (simple, undirected) graph $H_{n,k}$ whose vertices are all binary strings of length $n$, where two strings are adjacent if and only if they differ in exactly $k$ positions. Let $d_{n,k}$ be the number of connected components of $H_{n,k}$. Compute

$$\sum_{n=1}^{12} \sum_{k=1}^{n} d_{n,k}.$$

(For example, $H_{4,2}$ has two connected components.)

**Answer:** 4191
