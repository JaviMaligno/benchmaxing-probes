# hmmt2024-12 variant

A limping king is a game piece that can move from a cell to any cell sharing at least one vertex with it, except for the cells lying in the same column as its current cell.
A limping king starts in the top-left cell of a $9 \times 9$ board. Compute the maximum number of cells it can visit without visiting any cell twice (including its starting cell).

**Answer:** 73
