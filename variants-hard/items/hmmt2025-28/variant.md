Let $PQRS$ be an isosceles trapezoid with $PQ \parallel RS$ and $RS > PQ = 6$. Let $T$ be a point on line $RS$ such that $ST = 3$ and $S$ lies between $T$ and $R$. Let $N$ be the midpoint of $\overline{PT}$. Given that points $P, Q, R, S$, and $N$ all lie on a circle of radius $7$, compute $NS$.

**Answer:** $\dfrac{3\sqrt{6}}{2}$
