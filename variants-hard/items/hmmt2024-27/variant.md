# hmmt2024-27 variant

Let $XYZ$ be an acute triangle. Let $P, R$, and $S$ be the feet of the altitudes from $X, Y$, and $Z$ to sides $\overline{YZ}$, $\overline{ZX}$, and $\overline{XY}$, respectively, and let $T$ be the foot of the perpendicular from $X$ to line $RS$. Given that $XT=15$, $YZ=16$, and $XP=20$, compute the perimeter of triangle $PRS$.

**Answer:** $10\sqrt{7}$
