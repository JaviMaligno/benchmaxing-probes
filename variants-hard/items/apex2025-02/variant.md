# apex2025-02 — variant

A closed polygon $Q$ is drawn along the grid lines of an infinite sheet of squared paper, and $Q$ does not cross or touch itself. For $i=1,2,3$, let $b_i$ denote the number of unit cells of the sheet that have exactly $i$ of their four sides lying on the boundary of $Q$. Determine the largest real number $k$ such that the inequality
$$2b_1+3b_2>k\,b_3$$
holds for every such polygon $Q$.

**Answer:** $k=\dfrac{3}{2}$

(Original: $a_1+a_2>k a_3$, answer $1/2$.)
