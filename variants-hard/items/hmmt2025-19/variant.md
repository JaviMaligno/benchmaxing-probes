# hmmt2025-19 — variant

**Problem.** Two points are chosen independently and uniformly at random in the interior of a regular hexagon. Find the probability that the line through the two points meets two adjacent sides of the hexagon.

**Answer.** 1/18

(Original asked for a pair of opposite sides, answer 4/9. solve.py also gives 1/2 for sides separated by exactly one side, and the three cases sum to 1.)
