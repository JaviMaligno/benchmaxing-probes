# cmimc2025-36 variant

Consider a regular $24$-gon with vertices $B_1 B_2 \cdots B_{24}$, with indices taken modulo $24$ (so $B_i = B_{i+24}$). Two smaller regular $24$-gons will be constructed inside it.

In the first construction, draw every segment $\overline{B_i B_{i+10}}$; this produces $2$ stars, and a regular $24$-gon appears in the center. Call it the first $24$-gon.

In the second construction, draw every segment $\overline{B_i B_{i+11}}$; this produces a single star, and again a regular $24$-gon appears in the center. Call it the second $24$-gon.

Compute the ratio of the area of the first $24$-gon to the area of the second $24$-gon.

**Answer:** $2 + \dfrac{\sqrt{2}+\sqrt{6}}{2} = \dfrac{4+\sqrt{2}+\sqrt{6}}{2} \approx 3.931852$
