# hmmt2025-15 — variant

A museum floor is a $9 \times 13$ rectangular grid of rooms, and every pair of rooms sharing a wall is connected by a door. Priya wants to take a walk through the museum. She may start in any room, but she must finish in the room where she started, and she may not pass through any door more than once (not even in opposite directions). Compute the maximum number of doors she can pass through on such a walk.

**Answer:** 192
