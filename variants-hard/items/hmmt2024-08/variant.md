# hmmt2024-08 variant

Let $\omega=\cos \frac{2 \pi}{13}+i \sin \frac{2 \pi}{13}$. Suppose $a>b>c>d$ are positive **odd** integers satisfying
$$
\left|\omega^{a}+\omega^{b}+\omega^{c}+\omega^{d}\right|=\sqrt{3}.
$$

Compute the smallest possible value of $1000 a+100 b+10 c+d$.

**Answer:** 13931  (attained by $(a,b,c,d)=(13,9,3,1)$)
