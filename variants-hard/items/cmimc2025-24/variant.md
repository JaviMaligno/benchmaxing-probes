# cmimc2025-24 variant

Let $PQRSTU$ be a regular hexagon with side length $1$. Let $X$ be the point on side $\overline{RS}$ with $RX = \tfrac{1}{3}$, and let $Y$ be the unique point on side $\overline{ST}$ such that $PXYU$ is a trapezoid. Find the length of the altitude dropped from point $Y$ to $\overline{PX}$.

**Answer:** $\dfrac{3\sqrt{21}}{14}$
