# cmimc2025-15 variant

Consider a $12$-card deck consisting of three cards of each of the values $5, 6, 7$, and $8$ (each in a different color). A *pair* is defined as two cards directly next to each other in the deck with the same value. The deck is shuffled uniformly at random, and we scan it from left to right; whenever we encounter a pair, we discard all the cards up to that point (including the pair) and continue scanning from the next card. Let $N$ denote the number of times we discard cards. What is the expected value of $N$?

**Answer:** $\frac{20}{11}$
