Let $\Gamma$ be a circle with center $O$ and diameter $\overline{PQ}$, and let $PQRS$ be a cyclic quadrilateral inscribed in $\Gamma$, with $R$ and $S$ on the same side of line $PQ$. Suppose $PQ=20$, $QR=12$, and $PS=8$. Lines $QR$ and $PS$ meet at $T$. Let $J$ be the center of the $T$-excircle of triangle $TRS$. Find $OJ$.

**Answer:** $74-16\sqrt{21}$ (approx. 0.678789)
