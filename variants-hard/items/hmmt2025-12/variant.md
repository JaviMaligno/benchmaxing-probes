# hmmt2025-12 variant

Kelvin the frog sits on the bottom-left lily pad of a $3 \times 3$ grid of lily pads, and his home is the middle lily pad of the top row. He can only jump between two lily pads that are horizontally or vertically adjacent. Compute the number of ways to remove $4$ of the lily pads so that the bottom-left lily pad and the top-middle lily pad both remain, but Kelvin cannot get home.

**Answer:** 22
