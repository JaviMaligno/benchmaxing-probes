# hmmt2024-13 variant

**Problem.** Compute the number of ways to assemble $2$ blue unit cubes and $25$ yellow unit cubes into a $3 \times 3 \times 3$ cube such that blue is visible on exactly $3$ faces of the larger cube. (Rotations and reflections are considered distinct.)

**Answer.** $140$
