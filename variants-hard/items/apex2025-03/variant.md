# apex2025-03 variant

Let $\triangle PQR$ be an acute triangle with circumcircle $\Gamma$, and let $X$ be a point on segment $QR$. Circle $\gamma_1$ is tangent to segment $PX$, segment $QX$, and $\Gamma$. Circle $\gamma_2$ is tangent to segment $PX$, segment $RX$, and $\Gamma$, and both circles lie on the same side of $QR$ as $P$. If circles $\gamma_1$ and $\gamma_2$ have radii $4$ and $6$, respectively, and their centers are $11$ units apart, compute the sum of all possible values of the inradius of $\triangle PQR$.

**Answer:** $\frac{1170}{121}$
