# hmmt2024-03 variant

Compute the sum of all two-digit positive integers $m$ with the following property: for every four-digit (base 10) positive integer $\underline{p}\,\underline{q}\,\underline{r}\,\underline{s}$ that is a multiple of $m$, the number $\underline{q}\,\underline{r}\,\underline{s}\,\underline{p}$ (read in base 10) is also a multiple of $m$.

**Answer:** 143  (the valid $m$ are 11, 33, 99)
