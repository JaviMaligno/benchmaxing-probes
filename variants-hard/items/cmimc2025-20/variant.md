# cmimc2025-20 variant

For each positive integer $m$, let $c_m$ be the number of ways to write $m$ as an ordered sum of powers of $3$ (where $3^0=1$ counts as a power of $3$). For instance, $c_4=3$, because
$$4=1+1+1+1=1+3=3+1.$$
Let $r_m$ be the remainder when $c_m$ is divided by $3$. Compute
$$\sum_{m=1}^{3^{2030}} r_m .$$

**Answer:** 4122932
