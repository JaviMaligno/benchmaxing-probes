# hmmt2025-09 variant

Let $g$ be the unique polynomial of degree at most $2028$ such that for every $m \in \{1,2,3,\ldots,2029\}$,
$$
g(m)= \begin{cases}1 & \text{if } m \text{ is a perfect square} \\ 0 & \text{otherwise}\end{cases}
$$

Write the coefficient of $x^{2027}$ in $g$ as $\frac{p}{q}$, where $p$ and $q$ are integers with $\gcd(p,q)=1$. Find the unique integer $s$ with $0 \le s \le 2028$ such that $p - s q$ is divisible by $2029$. (Note that $2029$ is prime.)

**Answer:** 1069
