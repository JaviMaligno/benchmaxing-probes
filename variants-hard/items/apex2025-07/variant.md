# apex2025-07 variant

There are $2025$ students and $56$ colors, and each student owns exactly one marble of each color. For each student, the masses of their $56$ marbles are positive and sum to $1$. Find the smallest constant $C$ such that, no matter how the masses are distributed, each student can select one of their marbles so that, for every color, the total mass of the selected marbles of that color is at most $C$.

**Answer:** $\dfrac{703}{1060}$
