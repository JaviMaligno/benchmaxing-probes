Define the ratio of an ellipse to be the length of its major axis divided by the length of its minor axis.

Let $PQRS$ be a trapezoid with $PQ \parallel SR$ and $\angle PSR = 90^\circ$, where $PQ = 20$, $PS = 28$, and $SR = 110$. Find the smallest ratio of any ellipse that passes through all four vertices of $PQRS$.

**Answer:** $\frac{7}{2}$
