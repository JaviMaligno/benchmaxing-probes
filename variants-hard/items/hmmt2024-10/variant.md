# hmmt2024-10 variant

A polynomial $f \in \mathbb{Z}[x]$ is called *breakable* if and only if for every prime $p$, there exist polynomials $g_{p}, h_{p} \in \mathbb{Z}[x]$ with $\deg g_{p}, \deg h_{p} < \deg f$ such that all coefficients of $f - g_{p} h_{p}$ are divisible by $p$. Compute the sum of all positive integers $n \leq 100$ such that the polynomial $x^{4} + 20x^{2} + n$ is breakable.

**Answer:** 900
