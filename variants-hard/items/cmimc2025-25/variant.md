Let $\triangle PQR$ be an equilateral triangle. Let $\mathcal{E}_{PQ}$ be the ellipse with foci $P, Q$ consisting of all points whose distances to $P$ and $Q$ sum to $\tfrac{3}{2}PQ$, and in the parallel manner define $\mathcal{E}_{QR}$ and $\mathcal{E}_{PR}$. Let $\triangle XYZ$ be a (nondegenerate) triangle whose vertices are points where two of these ellipses intersect, such that the edges of $\triangle XYZ$ do not intersect those of $\triangle PQR$.
Compute the ratio of the largest side of $\triangle XYZ$ to the largest side of $\triangle PQR$.

**Answer:** $\dfrac{3+5\sqrt{3}}{8}$ (≈ 1.4575317547)
