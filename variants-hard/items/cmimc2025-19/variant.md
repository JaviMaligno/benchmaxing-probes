# cmimc2025-19 variant

A subset $T$ of $\{1,2,\ldots,20\}$ is chosen uniformly at random (all $2^{20}$ subsets equally likely). For $k=0,1,2,3,4$, let $q(k)$ denote the probability that $|T| \equiv k \pmod 5$.
Evaluate

$$
\sum_{k=0}^{4}\left|q(k)-\frac{1}{5}\right|
$$

**Answer:** $\frac{6119}{327680}$
