# apex2025-05 variant

Priya has a $1935 \times 1936$ rectangle in the coordinate plane, with sides parallel to the axes. She makes a cut from one side to another side which only goes down and/or right along grid lines. Then she rearranges the two pieces, possibly with rotations and/or reflections, without overlaps or gaps, to form a new rectangle which is not congruent to the original. How many possible new rectangles can she produce? (An $a \times b$ rectangle is considered the same as a $b \times a$ rectangle.)

**Answer:** 7

New rectangles: 968x3870, 1290x2904, 1452x2580, 1548x2420, 1720x2178, 1815x2064, 1892x1980.
