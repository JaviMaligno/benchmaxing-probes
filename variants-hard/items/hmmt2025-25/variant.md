Let $\triangle DEF$ be an equilateral triangle with side length $9$. Let $Q$ be a point inside $\triangle DEF$ such that $\angle EQF = 120^{\circ}$. The circle with diameter $\overline{DQ}$ meets the circumcircle of $\triangle DEF$ again at $Y \neq D$. Given that $DY = 7$, compute $YQ$.

**Answer:** $\sqrt{59} - 3\sqrt{3}$ (approx. 2.48499)
