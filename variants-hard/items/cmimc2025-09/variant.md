# cmimc2025-09 variant

**Problem.** Find the largest prime factor of $80^{5}-1$.

**Answer.** 5021

(Method as original: $80^5-1 = 79\cdot\Phi_5(80)$ and, with $x=80=5\cdot4^2$, the Aurifeuillean identity
$x^4+x^3+x^2+x+1=(x^2+3x+1)^2-5x(x+1)^2$ gives $\Phi_5(80)=(6641-1620)(6641+1620)=5021\cdot 8261$, $8261=11\cdot751$.)
