# cmimc2025-07 variant

Let $b_1 = 1$, and for each $k \geq 2$ let $b_k$ be the integer obtained by writing the units digit of $k$ at the right end of $b_{k-1}$ (so $b_{10}=1234567890$, $b_{11}=12345678901$). For a positive integer $m$, let $\nu_3(m)$ denote the exponent of the largest power of $3$ dividing $m$. Compute

$$\sum_{k=1}^{1080} \nu_3(b_k).$$

**Answer:** 1241
