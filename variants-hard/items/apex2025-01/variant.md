# apex2025-01 — variant

Let \( g: \mathbb{R} \to \mathbb{R} \) be a continuous function. Call a *chord* any horizontal segment (parallel to the x-axis) of integer length whose two endpoints lie on the graph of \( g \). Suppose the graph of \( g \) has exactly \( N \) chords, and one of them has length 2030. Determine the smallest possible value of \( N \).

**Answer:** 4059
