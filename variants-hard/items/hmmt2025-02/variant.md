# hmmt2025-02 variant

Priya writes the expression $\sqrt{\underline{a b c d e}}$ on the board, where $\underline{a b c d e}$ is a five-digit number and $a \neq 0$. Her little brother Theo moves the $a$ out front, changing the expression to $a \sqrt{\underline{b c d e}}$. Remarkably, the two expressions are equal. Compute the sum of all possible five-digit numbers $\underline{a b c d e}$.

**Answer:** 124875  (the possibilities are 33750 and 91125)
