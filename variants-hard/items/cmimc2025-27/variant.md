# Variant of cmimc2025-27

Let $ABC$ be a triangle with $AB=13$, $AC=15$, $BC=14$, and let $\overline{AD}$ be the altitude from $A$. A point $Q$ lies on segment $\overline{AD}$, strictly between $A$ and $D$. Let $\Gamma_1$ and $\Gamma_2$ be the circumcircles of triangles $AQB$ and $AQC$, respectively. Line $BC$ meets $\Gamma_1$ again at $B' \neq B$ and meets $\Gamma_2$ again at $C' \neq C$. Let $X \neq A$ be the point on $\Gamma_1$ with $B'X = B'A$, and let $Y \neq A$ be the point on $\Gamma_2$ with $C'Y = C'A$. Suppose that $X$, $Y$, and $A$ are collinear. Find the length $QA$.

**Answer:** $\dfrac{33}{4}$

(Q turns out to be the orthocenter, so $QA = 2R\cos A = 2\cdot\frac{65}{8}\cdot\frac{33}{65}$. Original 5-8-7 triangle: $2R\cos 60^\circ = R = \frac{7\sqrt3}{3}$.)
