# hmmt2025-14 variant

Theo starts at $(0,0)$ on a coordinate grid and wants to reach $(4,2)$. If Theo is at $(x, y)$, in a single step he can move to one of $(x+1, y)$, $(x, y+1)$, $(x-1, y+1)$, or $(x+1, y-1)$. He may not visit any point more than once, his $x$-coordinate must always stay between $0$ and $4$ inclusive, and his $y$-coordinate must always stay between $0$ and $2$ inclusive. Compute the number of ways for Theo to reach $(4,2)$.

**Answer:** 1600
