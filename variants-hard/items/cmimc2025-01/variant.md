# cmimc2025-01 — variant

Four runners line up at the same starting line for a $1$-mile race. When the race begins, runners Priya, Quinn, and Rosa each run at constant speeds of $12$ mph, $6$ mph, and $3$ mph, respectively. The fourth runner, Sam, starts out running at one third of Rosa's speed, but Sam has a superpower: his running speed instantly doubles every time another runner finishes the race. How many hours does it take for Sam to finish the race?

**Answer:** $\frac{11}{32}$
