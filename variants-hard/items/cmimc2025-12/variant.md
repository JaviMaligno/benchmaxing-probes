# cmimc2025-12 — variant

Every day, Coral the heron eats either $1$ or $2$ fish: it eats $1$ fish with probability $\tfrac{2}{3}$ and $2$ fish with probability $\tfrac{1}{3}$, independently of other days. Once Coral has eaten $8$ or more fish in total, its feathers will turn orange. Once Coral has eaten $9$ or more fish in total, it will get sick. What is the probability that Coral does not get sick on the day its feathers turn orange?

**Answer:** $\frac{4921}{6561}$
