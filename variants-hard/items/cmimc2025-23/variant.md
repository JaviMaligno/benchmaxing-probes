# cmimc2025-23 variant

Let $PQ$ be a segment of length $2$. Let $\odot P, \odot Q$ be circles with radius $\overline{PQ}$ centered at $P, Q$, and let $R, S$ be their intersection points. Draw circles $\odot R, \odot S$ with radius $\overline{RS}$, and let $T, U$ be the intersection points of $\odot R$ and $\odot S$. Draw circles $\odot T, \odot U$ with radius $\overline{TU}$ and let $V, W$ be their intersection points.

Choose the labels so that $T, P, Q, U$ occur in that order on their common line, and $R$ and $V$ lie on the same side of line $PQ$.

Compute the area of the pentagon $P R U V T$.

**Answer:** $7\sqrt{3}$
