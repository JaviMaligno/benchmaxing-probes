# cmimc2025-30 — variant

Let $\triangle ABC$ have $AB=5$, $BC=12$, $AC=13$, and let $\omega$ be its circumcircle. Let $P$ be a point on $\omega$ lying strictly on the arc between $B$ and $C$ that does not contain $A$ (so $P\neq B, C$). Drop perpendiculars from $P$ to $BC$ and $AC$, with feet $J$ and $Q$ respectively. Let $\ell$ be a line through $B$ meeting $AC$ at a point $K$. Let $M$ be the midpoint of $BQ$, let $CM$ meet $\ell$ at $I$, and let $AI$ meet $JQ$ at $U$. Suppose $B, J, U, M$ are concyclic. Let $\angle QJC=\theta$, and put $x=\cos\theta$, $y=\sin\theta$. They satisfy

$$169\,x y^{4}+120\,y^{3}=144\,x,$$

and numerically $x\approx 0.63995$, $y\approx 0.76842$.

Let line $BK$ meet $\omega$ again at $L$. Find $BL$, correct to two decimal places.

**Answer:** $11.52$ (exactly $BL = 12x+5y = 11.5215\ldots$)
