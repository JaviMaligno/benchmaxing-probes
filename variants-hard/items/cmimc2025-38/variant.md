# cmimc2025-38 variant

Let $S$ be the set of all complex numbers $m$ for which the four roots of
$$\left(x^{2}+4x+5\right)\left(x^{2}-2mx+25\right)=0$$
are concyclic in the complex plane. When the points of $S$ are plotted in the complex plane, $S$ is the union of finitely many curves. Find the total length of these curves.

**Answer:** $10+\dfrac{25\pi}{6}$
