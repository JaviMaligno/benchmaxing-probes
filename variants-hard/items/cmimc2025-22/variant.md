# cmimc2025-22 variant

Inside a cube with edge length $6$, eight balls of radius $\tfrac{3}{2}$ are placed so that each ball is externally tangent to exactly three of the others. Find the radius of the largest ball lying inside the cube that is externally tangent to all eight of these balls.

**Answer:** $\dfrac{3\sqrt{3}-3}{2}$ (≈ 1.0981)
