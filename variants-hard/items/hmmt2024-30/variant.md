# hmmt2024-30 variant

Point $Q$ lies inside the convex quadrilateral $WXYZ$, and

$$\angle QWX=\angle QZW,\quad \angle QWZ=\angle QZY,\quad \angle QXW=\angle QYX,\quad \angle QXY=\angle QYZ.$$

If $QW=3$, $QX=5$, and $QY=10$, compute the perimeter of $WXYZ$.

**Answer:** $\dfrac{18\sqrt{85}}{5}$ (about 33.1904)

Original: PA=4, PB=5, PC=10, answer 9√410/5. Same method: tangent circles make the quadrilateral an isosceles trapezoid with P on the midline.
