Let $PQRS$ be a convex trapezoid such that $\angle SPQ=\angle PQR=90^{\circ}$, $SP=3$, $PQ=4$, and $QR=9$. Let $\Gamma$ be a circle passing through $P$ and tangent to segment $\overline{RS}$ at point $X$. Suppose that the center of $\Gamma$ lies on line $QR$. Compute $RX$.

**Answer:** $3\sqrt{13}-2\sqrt{5}$ (about 6.3445)
