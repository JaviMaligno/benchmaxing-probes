# cmimc2025-26 variant

Points $P, Q, R, S, T$, and $U$ lie on a sphere with center $O$ and radius $r$ such that chords $\overline{PQ}$, $\overline{RS}$, and $\overline{TU}$ are pairwise perpendicular and all pass through a point $X$ inside the sphere. If $PX = 1$, $RX = 2$, $TX = 3$, and $OX = \sqrt{2}$, compute the sum of all possible values of $r^2$.


**Answer:** $\frac{412}{49}$
