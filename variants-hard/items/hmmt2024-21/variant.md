Inside an equilateral triangle of side length $9$, three congruent equilateral triangles of side length $x$, each with sides parallel to those of the large triangle, are placed in a pinwheel arrangement: each of the three has one vertex on a side of the large triangle (a different side for each) and another vertex lying on one of the other two congruent triangles. The three congruent triangles enclose a smaller equilateral triangle between them, and this central triangle has side length $2$. Compute $x$.

**Answer:** $\dfrac{7}{3}$
