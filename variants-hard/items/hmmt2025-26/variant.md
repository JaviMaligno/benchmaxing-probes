Trapezoid $PQRS$, with $PQ \parallel RS$, has side lengths $PQ=10$, $QR=7$, $RS=18$, and $SP=5$. Compute the area of the convex quadrilateral whose vertices are the circumcenters of $\triangle PQR$, $\triangle QRS$, $\triangle RSP$, and $\triangle SPQ$.

**Answer:** $\dfrac{21\sqrt{3}}{5}$
