Say that a semicircle is inscribed in another semicircle if the diameter of the smaller one is a chord of the larger one, and the arc of the smaller one is tangent to the diameter of the larger one.

Semicircle $\omega_1$ is inscribed in semicircle $\omega_2$, and $\omega_2$ is inscribed in semicircle $\omega_3$. The radius of $\omega_1$ is $3$, the radius of $\omega_3$ is $12$, and the diameters of $\omega_1$ and $\omega_3$ are parallel. Let $P$ and $Q$ be the endpoints of the diameter of $\omega_3$, and let the arc of $\omega_2$ touch segment $PQ$ at $T$. Compute $PT \cdot TQ$.

**Answer:** 72
