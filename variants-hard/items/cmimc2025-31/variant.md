# cmimc2025-31 variant

Call a date a "lucky date" if both the month number and the day number evenly divide the number formed by writing the month followed by the day. For example, June 12 is a lucky date, since both $6$ and $12$ divide $612$. However, June $9$ is not a lucky date, since $9$ does not divide $69$. How many lucky dates are there in June, July, and August combined?

**Answer:** 9
