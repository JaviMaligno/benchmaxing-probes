# hmmt2025-08 variant

Let $\operatorname{sgn}(x)$ equal $1$ if $x>0$, $-1$ if $x<0$, and $0$ if $x=0$. Evaluate

$$
\sum_{n=1}^{\infty} \frac{\operatorname{sgn}\left(\sin\left(3\cdot 2^{n}\right)\right)}{2^{n}}.
$$

(All angles are measured in radians.)

**Answer:** $1-\dfrac{6}{\pi} \approx -0.909859317$
