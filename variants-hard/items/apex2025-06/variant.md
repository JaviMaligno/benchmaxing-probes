# apex2025-06 — variant

For a positive integer $m$ and a prime $q$, let $f(m,q)$ denote the greatest nonnegative integer $f$ for which some polynomial $Q(x)$ with integer coefficients satisfies both:
- the coefficient of $x^m$ in $Q(x)$ equals $1$;
- $q^f$ divides $Q(x)$ for every integer $x$.

Compute
\[
\sum_{m=17}^{21} \sum_{q \in \{17,19\}} f(m,q)
\]
as an integer.

**Answer:** 198

(Original: n = 11..15, p in {11, 13}, answer 138.)
