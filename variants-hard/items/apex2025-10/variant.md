# apex2025-10 variant

Let $T$ be the set of all strings of length $18$ that use exactly six $1$s, six $2$s, and six $3$s. Call a string in $T$ *elegiac* if:

- no two adjacent characters are equal, and
- the string can be erased entirely by repeatedly deleting a contiguous substring equal to $123$, $231$, or $312$ (intermediate strings are allowed to contain adjacent equal characters).

How many elegiac strings are in $T$?

**Answer:** 792
