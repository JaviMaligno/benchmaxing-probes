# cmimc2025-37 variant

Recall that the binomial coefficient $\binom{n}{k}$ is the coefficient of $x^{k}$ in $(1+x)^{n}$. In the same spirit, define the trinomial coefficient $\binom{n}{k}_{3}$ to be the coefficient of $x^{k}$ in the expansion of $(1+x+x^{2})^{n}$.

How many integers $k$ with $0 \leq k \leq 4038$ satisfy $\binom{2019}{k}_{3} \equiv 1 \pmod{3}$?

**Answer:** 216
