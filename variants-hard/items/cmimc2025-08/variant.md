# cmimc2025-08 variant

Let $Q(x)=x^{4}+24x^{3}+65x^{2}-900x+2500$. It is known that $Q(x)>0$ for every real $x$.

The polynomial $Q$ has a root $z$ lying in the first quadrant of the complex plane, and $z$ can be written as $z=\frac{1}{2}\left(a+bi+\sqrt{c+di}\right)$, where $a, b, c, d$ are integers. Find $a+b+c+d$.

**Answer:** 388  ($a=-12,\ b=1,\ c=303,\ d=96$; $Q=(x^2+(12-i)x-40-30i)(x^2+(12+i)x-40+30i)$)
