Tomemos un hexágono regular de lado \(1\) y coordenadas:

\[
A=(1,0),\quad B=\left(\frac12,\frac{\sqrt3}{2}\right),\quad C=\left(-\frac12,\frac{\sqrt3}{2}\right),
\]
\[
D=(-1,0),\quad E=\left(-\frac12,-\frac{\sqrt3}{2}\right),\quad F=\left(\frac12,-\frac{\sqrt3}{2}\right).
\]

Como \(G\) es punto medio de \(\overline{CD}\):

\[
G=\left(\frac{-1/2-1}{2},\frac{\sqrt3/2+0}{2}\right)
=\left(-\frac34,\frac{\sqrt3}{4}\right).
\]

Sea \(H\in \overline{DE}\). Parametrizamos:

\[
H=D+t(E-D)=\left(-1+\frac t2,-\frac{\sqrt3}{2}t\right).
\]

Para que \(AGHF\) sea trapecio no degenerado, debe cumplirse \(AG\parallel HF\).

\[
\overrightarrow{AG}=G-A=\left(-\frac74,\frac{\sqrt3}{4}\right),
\]

\[
\overrightarrow{HF}=F-H=\left(\frac32-\frac t2,-\frac{\sqrt3}{2}+\frac{\sqrt3}{2}t\right)
=\left(\frac{3-t}{2},\frac{\sqrt3(t-1)}{2}\right).
\]

Igualando pendientes:

\[
\frac{\sqrt3(t-1)}{3-t}=\frac{\sqrt3/4}{-7/4}
\]

\[
\frac{t-1}{3-t}=-\frac17
\]

\[
7t-7=t-3
\]

\[
6t=4\implies t=\frac23.
\]

Por tanto,

\[
H=\left(-1+\frac13,-\frac{\sqrt3}{3}\right)
=\left(-\frac23,-\frac{\sqrt3}{3}\right).
\]

La recta \(AG\), que pasa por \(A(1,0)\), tiene vector director \((-7/4,\sqrt3/4)\). Su ecuación es:

\[
\sqrt3\,x+7y-\sqrt3=0.
\]

La distancia de \(H\) a \(AG\) es:

\[
d=\frac{\left|\sqrt3\left(-\frac23\right)+7\left(-\frac{\sqrt3}{3}\right)-\sqrt3\right|}
{\sqrt{3+49}}
\]

\[
=\frac{\left|-\frac{2\sqrt3}{3}-\frac{7\sqrt3}{3}-\sqrt3\right|}{\sqrt{52}}
\]

\[
=\frac{4\sqrt3}{2\sqrt{13}}
=\frac{2\sqrt3}{\sqrt{13}}
=\frac{2\sqrt{39}}{13}.
\]
