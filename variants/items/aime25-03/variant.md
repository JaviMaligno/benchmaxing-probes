# aime25-03 variant

The 10 members of a chess club went to a smoothie bar after their tournament. Each member ordered one smoothie: mango, banana, or kiwi. At least one member chose each flavor, and the number of members who chose mango was greater than the number who chose banana, which was greater than the number who chose kiwi. Let $N$ be the number of different assignments of flavors to members that meet these conditions. Find the remainder when $N$ is divided by $1000$.

**Answer:** 980  (N = 4980)
