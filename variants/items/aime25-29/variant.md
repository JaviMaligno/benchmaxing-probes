# aime25-29 variant

Let $\triangle PQR$ be a right triangle with $\angle P = 90^\circ$ and $QR = 4\sqrt{301}$. There exist points $X$ and $Y$ inside the triangle such that
$$PX = PY = QX = RY = XY = 26.$$
The area of the quadrilateral $QXYR$ can be expressed as $n\sqrt{3}$ for some positive integer $n$. Find $n$.

**Answer:** 345

(Legs: $PQ = 46$, $PR = 30\sqrt{3}$, or the mirror assignment; verified by solve.py.)
