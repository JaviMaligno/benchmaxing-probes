# aime25-14 variant

Let $PQRST$ be a convex pentagon with $PQ=38$, $QR=19$, $RS=48$, $ST=19$, $TP=38$, and $\angle Q=\angle T=60^{\circ}$. For each point $Y$ in the plane, define $g(Y)=PY+QY+RY+SY+TY$. The least possible value of $g(Y)$ can be expressed as $m+n\sqrt{p}$, where $m$ and $n$ are positive integers and $p$ is not divisible by the square of any prime. Find $m+n+p$.

**Answer:** 114  (minimum $= 74+37\sqrt{3}$)
