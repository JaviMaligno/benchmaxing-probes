# aime25-21 variant

Circle $\omega_1$ with radius $4$ centered at point $P$ is internally tangent at point $Q$ to circle $\omega_2$ with radius $20$. Points $R$ and $S$ lie on $\omega_2$ such that $\overline{QR}$ is a diameter of $\omega_2$ and $\overline{QR} \perp \overline{PS}$. The rectangle $KLMN$ is inscribed in $\omega_1$ such that $\overline{KL} \perp \overline{QR}$, $R$ is closer to $\overline{MN}$ than to $\overline{KL}$, and $S$ is closer to $\overline{LM}$ than to $\overline{KN}$. Triangles $\triangle SML$ and $\triangle RNM$ have equal areas. The area of rectangle $KLMN$ is $\frac{m}{n}$, where $m$ and $n$ are relatively prime positive integers. Find $m + n$.

**Answer:** 101 (area = 96/5)
