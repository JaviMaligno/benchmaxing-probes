# aime25-22 variant

Let $S$ be the set of positive integer divisors of $675$. A subset $T$ of $S$ is chosen uniformly at random. The probability that $T$ is nonempty and the least common multiple of its elements is $675$ is $\frac{m}{n}$, where $m$ and $n$ are relatively prime positive integers. Find $m + n$.

**Answer:** 117  (probability $53/64$)
