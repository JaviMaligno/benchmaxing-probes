# aime25-30 variant

There are exactly three positive real numbers $k$ such that the function
$$g(x) = \frac{(x - 8)(x - 50)(x - 72)(x - k)}{x}$$
defined over the positive real numbers attains its minimum value at exactly two positive real numbers $x$. Find the sum of these three values of $k$.

**Answer:** 182  (the values are $k = 2, 18, 162$)
