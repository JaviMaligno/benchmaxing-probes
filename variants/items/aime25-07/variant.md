# aime25-07 variant

The twelve letters $A$, $B$, $C$, $D$, $E$, $F$, $G$, $H$, $I$, $J$, $K$, and $L$ are split at random into six pairs. Within each pair the two letters are written side by side in alphabetical order, giving six two-letter words, and these six words are then arranged in alphabetical order. For instance, one possible outcome is $AC$, $BK$, $DE$, $FL$, $GJ$, $HI$. The probability that the last word in the list contains the letter $H$ is $\frac{m}{n}$, where $m$ and $n$ are relatively prime positive integers. Find $m+n$.

**Answer:** 893  (probability $= \frac{200}{693}$)
