# aime25-19 variant

The product
$$\prod_{k=3}^{80} \frac{\log_k \left(7^{k^2 - 1}\right)}{\log_{k+1} \left(7^{k^2 - 4}\right)} = \frac{\log_3 (7^{8})}{\log_4 (7^{5})} \cdot \frac{\log_4 (7^{15})}{\log_5 (7^{12})} \cdot \frac{\log_5 (7^{24})}{\log_6 (7^{21})} \cdots \frac{\log_{80} (7^{6399})}{\log_{81} (7^{6396})}$$
is equal to $\frac{m}{n}$, where $m$ and $n$ are relatively prime positive integers. Find $m + n$.

**Answer:** 673  (product = 632/41)
