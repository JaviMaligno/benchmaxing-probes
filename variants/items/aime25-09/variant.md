# aime25-09 variant

The parabola with equation $y = x^2 - 5$ is rotated $60^\circ$ counterclockwise about the origin. The original parabola and its image meet in exactly one point of the fourth quadrant, and that point has $y$-coordinate $\frac{a - \sqrt{b}}{c}$, where $a$, $b$, and $c$ are positive integers and $a$ and $c$ are relatively prime. Find $a + b + c$.

**Answer:** 74  (y = (3 - sqrt(69))/2)
