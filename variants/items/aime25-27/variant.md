# aime25-27 variant

Let $B_1 B_2 B_3 \ldots B_{11}$ be a non-convex simple polygon with $11$ sides satisfying the following conditions:

- For every integer $2 \le i \le 10$, the triangle $B_i B_1 B_{i+1}$ has area $1$.
- For every integer $2 \le i \le 10$, $\cos(\angle B_i B_1 B_{i+1}) = \frac{4}{5}$.
- The perimeter of the polygon $B_1 B_2 \dots B_{11}$ is $20$.

Then $B_1 B_2 + B_1 B_{11}$ can be written as $\frac{m\sqrt{n} - p}{q}$, where $m, n, p, q$ are positive integers, $n$ is not divisible by the square of any prime, and no prime divides all of $m, p, q$. Find $m + n + p + q$.

**Answer: 119** ($B_1B_2 + B_1B_{11} = \frac{9\sqrt{85} - 5}{20}$)
