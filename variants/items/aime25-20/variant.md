# aime25-20 variant

In $\triangle PQR$, $\angle QPR = 80^\circ$, $\angle PQR = 58^\circ$, and $\angle PRQ = 42^\circ$. Let $D$, $E$, and $F$ be the midpoints of sides $\overline{QR}$, $\overline{PR}$, and $\overline{PQ}$, respectively. The circumcircle of $\triangle DEF$ meets $\overline{QD}$, $\overline{PE}$, and $\overline{PF}$ again at points $G$, $H$, and $J$, respectively. The points $G$, $D$, $E$, $H$, $J$, and $F$ divide the circumcircle of $\triangle DEF$ into six minor arcs. Find $\wideparen{DE} + 2 \cdot \wideparen{HJ} + 3 \cdot \wideparen{FG}$, where the arcs are measured in degrees.

**Answer:** 416

(arcs: DE = 84, HJ = 40, FG = 84; verified by solve.py, which reproduces 336 on the original 84/60/36 triangle)
