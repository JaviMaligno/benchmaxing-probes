# aime25-12 variant

The points of $3$-dimensional coordinate space that lie in the plane $x+y+z=87$ and whose coordinates satisfy
$$x-yz<y-zx<z-xy$$
form three disjoint convex regions, exactly one of which has finite area. This area can be written as $a\sqrt{b}$, where $a$ and $b$ are positive integers and $b$ is not divisible by the square of any prime. Find $a+b$.

**Answer:** 678  (area $= 675\sqrt{3}$)
